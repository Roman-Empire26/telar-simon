---
title: "Education Defines the Public and Social Legitimacy of the State "
---

"Lo que no es JENERAL sin excepion no es verdaderamente PUBLICO y lo que no es PUBLICO no es SOCIAL"
"What is not GENENRAL without expection in not truly PUBLIC and what is not PUBLIC is not SCOIAL"
Rodriquez establishes this chain of logic linking education to public governance. For instance, a government can't claim that its educational systems and policies are public and social if their educational systems and policies only reach a select few. Public education must be universal, without universal education being provided to the whole population, with the deliberate techniques, states and society won't be able to reach their full potential as a government and society. There won't be any growth for the people, for the state.
