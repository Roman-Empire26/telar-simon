---
title: "Education Forms Social Conduct, While Instruction Accumulates Knowledge"
---

Rodriguez makes an important distinction when he says "Instruir no es EDUCAR." For him to simply teach someone a skill without the proper education to follow is not a good precedent to set. It gives a false sense of empowerment and doesn't fully prepare a person to participate in society. He explains that a person can be a soldier without knowing military strategy, the same way a person can be given instruction without being properly educated as well. That education has a much higher social purpose.
