Universal education must include everyone without exception because a republic depends on educated citizens and shares social customs. Education therefore becomes the Foundation that produces the citizens, customs, and collective understanding necessary for a functioning and legitmate government. 
Rodriquez does not see government as something that simply impose laws on people. A republican government requires educated citizens, who understand society, participate in it, and can act for the General Advantage

Education develops customs, and these customs create the social foundation necessary for legitmate authority. In a true republic, Roqriguez argues that authority should come from customs and the collective will, rather than from one person's will or imposed power 

Society operates as an organic system....
