---
title: "Government Decrees Require the General Means of Execute Them"
---

"Extender con arte será, no solo hacer que" 
"To extend with art will be, not only to make"
Rodríguez emphasizes that true governing (extending with art) requires two steps. Making all citizens aware of what is ordered and providing, in general, the practical educational means so citizens can actually carry out those orders. Education is connected to the government through movement. At least according to Simon Rodriguez. He believes education, politics, and writing are a medium of "thought," which is how they unite.
